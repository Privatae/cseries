Your recovery key

Every backup of your Host is encrypted to your recovery key, and opens only with it. Your browser makes it, here, and shows it to you once; your Host receives only its public half, which opens nothing. Keep the key somewhere away from this machine: on paper, or in a password manager.

Your bastion's copy

                    
A bastion on a machine you own can keep its own encrypted copy of your memory, which only you can open: your bastion encrypts it on its own machine, the encrypted copy is kept in this Host’s database, and this Host never holds the keys. A different copy from the backups above.
