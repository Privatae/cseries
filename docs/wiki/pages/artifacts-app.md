Artifacts is the window where you see the live pages your Host builds for you in conversation: a mockup, a plan, a report, even a whole desktop. It comes with every Host. When your Host changes an artifact, the window updates while you watch. Each one is shown in a sealed frame that has no access to your account.

## How do I get an artifact?

Ask your Host to sketch or show you something. It builds the artifact in the conversation and can open it on your screen. The gallery shows every artifact as a live, scaled-down preview, newest change first, with a search box and a filter for ordinary artifacts or desktops. An artifact can show pictures your Host has made, such as images it generated; they are put into the page each time it is shown, so the sealed frame still reaches nothing outside.

## How do I show my Host what to change?

Turn on **Tune**. Click any part of the page and a reference to that exact element goes into your next message, so your Host knows which one you mean. Double-click a piece of text to change it yourself: the edit is made to the page, and your next message tells your Host what you changed.

## What if an artifact has several pages?

If an artifact holds several pages and none of them is the main one, nothing opens on its own. The window lists the pages and you pick one. Once a page is open, a drop-down in the top bar switches between them.

## Can I compare versions?

When your Host makes several variants of a design, they appear as tabs. **Use this one** makes the variant you picked the main version straight away, without another round of conversation.

## Can I undo a change?

Yes. **History** lists the revisions: every change your Host makes and every edit you make is kept as one. Click a revision to see how the page looked then, and **Restore** to bring it back. A restore is itself a revision, so it can be undone too. Every revision of each artifact is kept, with every file in it; the oldest are removed only when your Host's disk cannot take a new one.

## Can an artifact become my desktop?

Yes, if it is built as one. **Use as desktop** puts the open artifact on your screen as the desktop itself. For a page that cannot be a desktop the button stays off, and pointing at it tells you why. The page is opened once in a real browser first; one that does not render is refused, and the window shows the error and the line it happened on. [Artifacts and designs](wiki:artifacts-and-designs) covers designs and desktops in full.
