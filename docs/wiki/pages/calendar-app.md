Calendar is one calendar for two kinds of thing: your own events, and the work your Host has set itself to do later. It comes with every Host, so there is nothing to install. Your dock gets it next to Circles once, even if you have arranged your dock; take it out and it stays out. While it is in your dock, its icon counts what is still scheduled for the rest of today.

## What is on it?

- **Your Host's tasks.** When you ask your Host to do something later, or on a schedule ("check the backups every morning"), it sets a task, and the task shows here, marked as your Host's, with when it runs next and how often it repeats.
- **Your own events**, which you add with + Event.
- **Your Host's log** of things that happened, such as an app being installed.

The month shows each event on its day. Beside it, or below it on a phone, is a list you choose: what is coming up, today, everything, only your Host's tasks, or one kind of event. Pick a day on the month to list just that day.

## What did a task do?

Open a task to see what it does, in the words it was set with, and every time it ran: when, whether it worked, and the start of what it reported, or the error it hit. The newest runs come first, twenty at a time.

## How do I stop a task?

Open it and choose Cancel task. You can add why, and your Host can read your reason. A cancelled task does not run again. If it is running at that moment, it stops before its next step and does not report back.

A request your Host is keeping until you next write to it shows as held. It runs with your next message, and there is nothing to do here.

## How do I add an event?

Choose + Event, give it a title and a time, and save. The time is on your own clock. You can mark your own event done, or cancel it, later.

Circles still shows the same calendar beside your conversation. See [Circles](wiki:circles-app).
