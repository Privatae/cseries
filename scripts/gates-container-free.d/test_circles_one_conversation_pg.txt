# Circles is one conversation: a blank login opens on a greeting with no thread tabs; two topics run their turns at once and words for the running topic go into it; a topic last active 14 days ago behind 14 newer ones is found and its history is the turn's; a root-thread message is never picked and the root thread is never offered; picking is conservative (a near miss starts a new topic with none of the other's turns, a clear continuation joins, a short follow-on stays, a failed pick starts a new topic, a switch leaves a marker saying which way it went); the feed reads every topic as one conversation, page by page; related topics are one-line pointers; after a turn the topic's overflow summary is written to the database past its budget and read by the topic's next history, never for the root thread.
