# A nudge that arrives as a turn ends reaches the Host exactly once. The real EntityRuntime.send_message with a scripted model and a stub pool, the words put in through the real TurnRegistry.nudge on the turn's own session id: taken while the turn's last model call streams, the loop's exit reads them in one more round and sends the nudge_read receipt (once, never again as a message); arriving after the turn released its holds, the registry refuses them and the page sends them as the next message (once). The receipt carries the words stripped, so the page holds, echoes and sends a nudge trimmed: words typed with a leading newline are settled by it and not sent a second time. No pod, no DB, no model.
