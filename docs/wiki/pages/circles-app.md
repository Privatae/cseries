Circles is your everyday conversation with your Host: catching up, thinking out loud, asking questions, with no project attached. It comes with every Host. The window is a deck of cards, one for each contact.

## What is on the cards?

- **Your card.** Its back is your Host: the conversation, and the threads beside it as tabs, which you can start, rename, close, clear, delete, or split where the talk turned. Deleting a thread also removes what your Host learned in it. While your Host works, a line above the box says so, with what it is doing and for how long. Tap it to see everything running, and any background work that failed. **Where this stands** stays one line until you tap it. Its front is how people see you: your interests, who can reach you, the machines you are on, and your number.
- **A card for each person you know.** The front is your chat with that person. Your Host does not read it unless you turn on the reading switch on that card, and the person is told when you do. The back is their Host, and your topics with it.
- **Other cards**: a Host you know with no person, a room of Hosts, a group (its roster and votes), and **Support**.
- **Waiting on you**: the decisions your Host is waiting on, and who is at the door. Every decision, decided ones too, opens in full from there.
- **At the door**: requests to connect, requests to join your groups, introductions from shared interests, and Hosts found on your machines.
- **Coming up**: your next events, from the [Calendar app](wiki:calendar-app).

**All your contacts**, under your cards, lists everyone you hold, the blocked ones too, and **Message** beside a contact opens their card. A contact's card has its notes, Block and Remove.

## Where did the inbox, calendar and address book go?

The address book is the cards themselves, and the inbox is At the door and Waiting on you; notices to you are on the Support card. The calendar is its own app, [Calendar](wiki:calendar-app). How your Host thinks and replies is in [Settings](wiki:settings-app), under Response Shaping. Who may reach you, its rules, and your machines are in the [Network app](wiki:network-app). Searching everything your Host can see is in the [Context app](wiki:context-app).

## How do I control who can message me?

In the [Network app](wiki:network-app), which has a setting for who may message you. **Open** lets anyone message you, and per-sender rules can still block someone. **Contacts only** lets through only your contacts and senders you have allowed. **Closed** keeps messages from other Hosts out entirely. Your card's front says which you chose. To block one person or one Host, use Block on their card. Messages between Hosts need a platform connection that a C-Series Host does not have, on its own or on the Privatae network, so a C-Series Host receives none.

## What happens when I report a problem?

A C-Series Host cannot send a support ticket to Privatae. The Support card says so and shows where a request can go instead. [Getting help](wiki:getting-help) says more.

## When should I use the Workbench instead?

When you want to build something. Circles is for conversation; once you settle on something concrete to build, your Host can open it as a project in the [Workbench](wiki:workbench-app) and carry the conversation over.
