# Before Circles' book polls its lists: GET /api/circles/threads and GET /api/circles/conversations on a real kernel schema holding 300 threads, 100,000 turn rows and 25,000 messages from 200 peer Hosts and 100 people. The thread list reads each thread's newest row, newest reply and unseen reply through v219's two thread indexes, and the original conversation's count and marks through its two, never a scan of entity_memory (custom and generic plans); the per-thread reads touch at most 12 buffers a thread and the original conversation's at most 400; a 2 s wall-clock ceiling is the backstop. Measured on the build machine: the thread list's median went from 216 ms on main 104e742ac to 56 ms, about 81 s without v219's two thread indexes (measured once, by hand); the conversations list 12 ms to 17 ms. Fresh clone of the kernel schema on TEST_PG_DSN / PEER_TEST_DSN / TEST_DSN, else a docker throwaway.
