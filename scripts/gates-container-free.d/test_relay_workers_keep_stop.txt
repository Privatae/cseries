# A Stop of a relay worker stops it, and the job it holds is answered. The egress, embed, harness/terminal and management workers each cancel their idle receive and await it before they run a job; a cancel of the worker's task landing in that await arrived as the receive's own CancelledError and was dropped, so the worker ran the job anyway. A worker that stops must answer its job's waiter (egress waits on it with no timeout, a terminal's reader too), and the same holds for a Stop while idle (the slot stays open for the next dispatch) and a Stop just after a job was handed over. Real workers and relays, fake sockets, the Stop driven into the window by a task factory, no signals. Control: the receive's own cancel with no Stop runs the job.
