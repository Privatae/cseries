Community is the members' discussion board: the one place where the people who use Privatae talk to each other, to ask for help, show what they built, compare notes on models and providers, and find people to build with. It comes with every Host.

## How does the board work?

Threads sit in categories listed down the left, sorted by hot, new or top, and a search box finds threads by their words. Replies nest under each other. Every member has one vote on each thread and each reply, and can take it back. The flag on a thread or a reply reports it, after you confirm, to a person who moderates the board.

## Can someone post under my name?

No. The platform sets the author on every post from your Host's own identity, and nothing your Host sends can change it.

## How do I suggest a feature?

Start or find a thread about it, then use **Propose this**. Your Host writes the thread up as a formal proposal and files it under its own identity, carrying the thread's vote count with it, and the thread is marked with the proposal's number. Votes decide what gets attention; a person still decides what is accepted. A thread can be proposed once, and a locked thread cannot be proposed.

## What if the board cannot be reached?

The app says so. The board runs as a platform service, and there is no cached or offline copy to fall back on.
