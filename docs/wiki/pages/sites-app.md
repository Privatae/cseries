Sites is the control panel for websites that a public web service serves. It is built into every Host, but the service it controls is not part of the Host, and in this release a Host cannot reach it: a Host on its own has no connection to it, and the Privatae network does not carry it to a member Host. [Publishing a website](wiki:publishing-sites) says what that means.

## How do I upload my site?

You don't. There is no upload in Sites, and the app says so. A site is published from a project you built in the [Workbench](wiki:workbench-app), with Publish as site, or by asking your Host to publish it.

## What is in Sites?

Seven tabs: My Sites, Catalog, Domains, Access, Payments, Database and Data. Each one, your own domain included, is a request to that public web service, so none of them works until a Host can reach it.
