# error_events had recorded nothing for two months on any Host while tool errors happened daily. The recorder records a failed host tool result (error / denied / blocked / unavailable, never ok, dispatched, not_found, ungrounded or a dedup repeat), keeps the error whole up to 16 KiB and says how much was left out past it, schedules the write off the tool loop, and never raises. The turn's call site is checked too: its recorder call never sits under the never-wired `if _prompt_console:`. Stdlib only; no pod, no DB.
