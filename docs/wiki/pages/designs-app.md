Designs is a shelf of complete desktops, the whole look of your Host's screen, that you can look at and wear. It comes with every Host.

## What is on the shelf?

Two shelves:

- **Ours** holds the house designs. Two come with every Host, **First Light** and **Garnet Silk**, and are there with no connection to anything. A Host with a platform connection also sees the desktops published to the shared design library for everyone.
- **Yours** holds the desktops made on this Host.

The shared library is a Privatae service that a Host reaches through a platform connection. A Host on its own has none, and the Privatae network does not offer it, so on such a Host **Ours** shows the two built-in designs and **Yours** shows the desktops made on this Host.

Each card is the real design running live in a sealed-off preview frame, not a screenshot.

## How do I try one?

Click **Try it** and the design goes on your screen. First it is opened once in a real browser, the way your screen would show it, which takes a little longer when that browser has to start. While you are trying it you can **Keep it** or **Go back**. Go back puts back the desktop you had, and what your phone showed, and throws away the trial copy, so trying ten designs does not leave ten copies behind.

Trying makes a copy on your own Host. From then on the two are unrelated: if the author changes or removes theirs, your screen does not change.

## How do I make and share my own?

Ask your Host, in conversation, to design a new desktop. It appears under Yours as soon as it exists. **Publish** sends it to the shared library, which needs that same platform connection. You can remove a design you published; Hosts already wearing it keep their own copy.

Every design is checked with the same rules your desktop uses, both when it is published and when it is tried, so the shelf never offers one your desktop would refuse. It is also opened in a real browser before it is published and before it is tried: a design that stops with an error, crashes, leaves the page or keeps reloading itself, shows nothing at all, or leaves out the apps or the bar it promises is refused, and the refusal names the exact error and the line it happened on. The browser opens each design a second time at phone size, and one that breaks there is refused too. A design is only published once that browser check has run. Trying one when no browser can be started still works, unless that exact design already failed the check.
