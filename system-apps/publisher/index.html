How to publish
Publish from the Workbench: your host packs the project from its repo and submits it here. Drop a .cseries bundle the platform built below if you have one. A zip assembled by hand carries no build record and is refused — the store distributes what the platform packed from a committed repo, so every installer can verify it. Your request then goes to human review — every publish is reviewed for safety, quality, and duplicates before it appears in the store. You'll see the decision (and the reason, if declined) under Publish requests below. The store is free at launch, so apps publish at no price.
